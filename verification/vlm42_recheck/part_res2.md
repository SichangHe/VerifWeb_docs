## WRONG / OVERSTATED

1. "the way WebAssembly's spec now generates its own Coq/Isabelle definitions" -- WRONG (overstated).
 Source: https://github.com/WebAssembly/spec/blob/main/spectec/README.md. Quote: "Because this DSL can transport sufficient domain knowledge, various artefacts could be generated through dedicated backends: ... the _Coq_ and _Isabelle_ definitions for mechanisation". It says "could be generated", a plan.
 Repo check (gh api, main tree): spectec/src has backend-ast, backend-interpreter, backend-latex, backend-prose, backend-splice; no Coq/Rocq/Isabelle backend anywhere under spectec; the only file under spectec mentioning Isabelle is the README. WasmCert-Coq/Isabelle are separate hand-maintained repos.
 Correct text: "WebAssembly's spec is written in a DSL (SpecTec) that generates the spec's LaTeX and prose and a reference interpreter; Coq/Isabelle backends are listed as a goal, and I found none in the repo."

## CONFIRMED (with nuance)

2. S&P 2022 quote -- confirmed verbatim. Source: Ghasemisharif, Kanich, Polakis, "Towards Automated Auditing for Account and Session Management Flaws in SSO Deployments", abstract (paper_collection PDF, pdftotext): "Our study also shows that less than 2% of RPs correctly react to SSO revocation and 67% continue to allow account access even 10 days after revocation." IdP is Facebook per paper.
3. "85% of 520 sites don't invalidate tokens after logout (thesis, 2024)" -- confirmed. Lamisa, Concordia MASc 2024 (paper_collection copy), contributions: "446 out of 520 websites (85%) tested for JWT invalidation do not invalidate JWTs included in requests after user logout." Nuance: body says "do not invalidate the JWT for at least one of the requests after the user is logged out"; the 520 are sites using JWT where logout could be automated, not 520 random sites. (concordia URL itself returns 403 to curl.)
