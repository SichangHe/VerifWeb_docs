# Memory prices and device memory constraints

(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)

Evidence for the human's claim: "memory prices have been skyrocketing, and they are forecasted to keep rising."
Retrieved 2026-11-20. Quotes are verbatim from the linked page; some are trimmed excerpts.

## Takeaway

- fact: DRAM contract prices rose ~80% QoQ in 2Q26 and ~40% in 3Q26 (TrendForce)
- forecast: increases continue but slow through 2027; makers say shortage lasts to 2028–2030
- fact/forecast: phones and PCs get pricier and lower-end devices get less RAM
- unknown: no reliable current share of users on ≤4 GB devices found

## Forecasts

4. TrendForce, 2026-10-20 (same link): "Fourth-quarter DRAM contract prices are expected to rise about 20% quarter-on-quarter." Linked headline on that page: "2027 Price Outlook Revised Upward with Blended ASP Forecast to Rise 121% YoY"
5. IDC via TechCrunch, 2026-02-27, [link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/memory-shortage-could-cause-the-biggest-smartphone-shipments-dip-in-over-a-decade/):
   - "IDC predicts that this will cause smartphone shipments to plummet by 12.9% this year."
   - "smartphone ASP [average selling price] is projected to rise 14% to a record $523 this year."
   - IDC's Nabila Popal: rising component costs could make the sub-$100 smartphone "permanently uneconomical."
   - "IDC added that it expects RAM prices to stabilize by mid-2027."
6. SK hynix (secondary, same Sovereign link): shortage "expected to persist through 2030"; CEO to Reuters: 2027 likely "the worst year in the industry's history from the supply perspective." Primary not checked.
